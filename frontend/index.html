User Manual

Translate Audio

Click Start Recording and speak a Na'vi word or phrase. Click Stop Recording when done. The system transcribes your speech and translates it to English.

You can also upload a pre-recorded audio file (WAV, MP3, or OGG, max 30 seconds).

Translate Text

Type any Na'vi text in the text box and click Translate. You'll get the English translation plus a word-by-word breakdown showing which words were found in the dictionary.

Contribute

Help improve the translator by submitting new Na'vi words. Enter the Na'vi word, its English meaning, and optionally record the pronunciation. Submissions are reviewed before being added to the training data.

Pipeline Status

View the health of the API, model loading status, and links to the management consoles (MLflow, Airflow, Prometheus, Grafana).

About

Na'vi Translator is an MLOps project for DA6360. It uses Whisper for speech recognition and MarianMT for translation, with automated retraining when data drift is detected.
